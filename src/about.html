About Soil Nurse

Soil Nurse is a smart soil health platform designed to help you understand, care for, and improve your soil with ease. Whether you're a beginner gardener, student, or future agritech innovator, Soil Nurse turns complex soil data into simple, actionable insights.

We believe healthy plants start from healthy soil and Soil Nurse is here to “nurse” your soil back to its best condition.

What We Do

Soil Nurse help you:

Analyze soil conditions like pH, moisture, and nutrients (NPK).

Get personalized recommendations for plant care.

Match plants with the most suitable soil environment.

Track soil health over time in one simple dashboard.

Why Soil Nurse?

Instead of guessing what your soil needs, Soil Nurse gives you clear guidance based on data. It’s like having a digital agronomist in your pocket always ready to help your plants thrive.

We combine science, simplicity, and technology to make soil care more accessible for everyone.
